## 01 — Problem

Every city has a real-time social pulse — where people are actually gathering, what's building momentum, what's dead — and almost none of it is legible from your phone. You find out an hour late, from a story someone else posted.

## 02 — Origin

Started as a question: what if "what's happening tonight" was a live system instead of a group chat rumor.

## 03 — Thesis

A city interface that surfaces where collective energy is forming, in real time, without turning nightlife into another feed to doomscroll.

Active build. Full architecture notes and screenshots land here as the product matures.
