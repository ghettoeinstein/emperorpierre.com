## 01 — Problem

Most "free credit tools" are lead-gen funnels wearing a helpful mask — you trade your data for a number that exists to sell you something else.

## 02 — Thesis

Build the useful version. Minimal data collection, no dark patterns, no sale of your information as the actual business model.

## 03 — Type

Open-source-leaning consumer infrastructure — small, boring, useful.
